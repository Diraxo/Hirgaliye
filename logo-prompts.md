# Hirgaliye Logo — AI Generation Prompts

## Option A — Wordmark only (recommended)
This matches what's already built on the actual website: clean, minimal,
no illustrated icon. Studios like Vercel and Linear use this approach —
it ages better than a gradient icon and looks more premium, not less.

Prompt:
"Minimalist wordmark logo, the text 'HIRGALIYE', modern geometric
sans-serif typography (like Space Grotesk or Inter), clean sharp letterforms,
solid white or light gray color, on a pure black background, no icon, no
illustration, no gradient effects, no circuit lines, no decorative elements —
just refined confident typography. Small teal (#00E5C7) accent mark or dot
to the left of the wordmark only, nothing else. Flat, modern, tech studio
brand identity, ultra minimal, lots of negative space, professional and
restrained, not busy."

## Option B — If you still want an icon
A single abstract mark, not a literal object (no monitor/phone clipart),
and not a generic "gradient letter + circuit squiggle" template.

Prompt:
"Abstract minimalist logo mark, a single continuous line forming an upward
arrow or ascending path, representing an idea becoming real, flat solid
color teal (#00E5C7) on black background, no gradient, no drop shadow, no
circuit board decoration, no floating nodes, geometric and simple like a
Swiss design pictogram, single shape, bold and confident, scales down to a
small favicon cleanly, modern tech studio brand mark."

## Option C — Profile picture / avatar (Facebook, TikTok, etc.)
Wordmarks don't survive a circular crop — a wide word gets chopped down to
2-3 letters at avatar size. Use a monogram instead, in the exact same style
as the wordmark, so it reads as the same brand everywhere.

Prompt:
"Minimalist monogram logo, a single bold letter 'H', modern geometric
sans-serif typeface (like Space Grotesk), solid white color, centered on a
pure black square background, small solid teal (#00E5C7) circle/dot
positioned to the upper-left of the H like a tittle or accent mark, generous
padding around the letter so nothing touches the edges, flat design, no
gradient, no shadow, no 3D effect, no circuit lines or decoration — clean
and legible at very small sizes, works well cropped into a circle."

Specs to request/crop to: 500×500px minimum, square canvas, keep the H and
dot centered and well within a circle inscribed in the square (about 80% of
the canvas) so nothing gets clipped by the circular crop Facebook/TikTok/etc.
apply automatically.
